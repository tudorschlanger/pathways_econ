% ---------- Background ----------
\begin{definitionbox}[House Bill 5035]
  A \textbf{bell-to-bell} phone ban in all Connecticut public schools.
  Students may bring phones but must store them during the school day (in a backpack, locker, or Yondr pouch---each district chooses).
\end{definitionbox}

\vspace{0.5em}

\textbf{Key Facts}
\begin{itemize}
  \item New Haven pilot: \textbf{\$375,000} for Yondr pouches across 10 schools (5,800 students).
  \item That's roughly \textbf{\$65 per student} if pouches are used.
  \item Funded by \textbf{school districts}---no dedicated state funding in the bill.
  \item The bill passed the CT House with strong bipartisan support (\textbf{117--31}).
  \item Average U.S.\ teen spends \textbf{4.8 hours/day} on their phone (Common Sense Media, 2023).
\end{itemize}

  \midrule
  Opportunity Cost & The \textbf{value} of the \textbf{next best alternative} when you make a decision. Includes both direct costs (money spent) and foregone benefits. \\[0.3em]
  Sunk Cost & A cost you \textbf{cannot recover}---money, time, or effort already spent and gone forever. \\[0.3em]
  Sunk Cost Fallacy & Letting past, unrecoverable costs influence your \textbf{future} decisions---staying on a path just because you've already invested in it. \\

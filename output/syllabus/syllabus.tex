\section*{About This Workshop}

This workshop uses real-life and policy examples to show how economics shapes the world around you --- from everyday choices to climate change, global trade, and financial markets. No prior economics knowledge required.

% =============================================================================
% Course Schedule
% =============================================================================
\section*{Course Schedule}

\textit{90 minutes per session}

% ---------- Day 1 ----------
\noindent\textbf{Day 1 --- Economic Decision-Making \& Everyday Choices}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Concepts:} scarcity, trade-offs, opportunity cost, the sunk-cost fallacy, cost-benefit analysis
  \item \textbf{Activity:} Stakeholder Analysis --- argue a real policy from the perspective of students, teachers, and government officials
  \item \textbf{Policy debate:} Should Connecticut schools ban cell phones bell-to-bell? (HB 5035)
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.6em}

% ---------- Day 2 ----------
\noindent\textbf{Day 2 --- Correlation vs.\ Causation}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Concepts:} correlation vs.\ causation, confounding variables, reverse causality, causal diagrams (DAGs)
  \item \textbf{Puzzle:} untangle a classic confounder --- why do ice cream sales and deaths rise together?
  \item \textbf{Activity:} Chart Detective --- analyze real misleading charts, then draw the same data two ways
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.6em}

% ---------- Day 3 ----------
\noindent\textbf{Day 3 --- Climate Change \& Common Resources}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Concepts:} supply and demand, rivalrous and excludable goods, externalities, tragedy of the commons, government policy tools
  \item \textbf{Game:} Candy Commons --- experience the tragedy of the commons, with and without monitoring
  \item \textbf{Policy debate:} Should the U.S.\ put a price on carbon? (a \$50/ton carbon tax)
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.6em}

% ---------- Day 4 ----------
\noindent\textbf{Day 4 --- Trade \& Tariffs}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Concepts:} gains from trade, absolute vs.\ comparative advantage, specialization, tariffs and who pays them
  \item \textbf{Worked example:} A Tale of Two Countries --- specialize and swap to see why trade grows the pie
  \item \textbf{Policy debate:} Are the 2018 U.S.\ steel tariffs worth it? Weigh benefits and costs from real sources
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.6em}

% ---------- Day 5 ----------
\noindent\textbf{Day 5 --- Financial Literacy}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Concepts:} compound interest, inflation and real vs.\ nominal returns, bonds and interest rates, stocks, diversification
  \item \textbf{Activity:} Financial Literacy Quiz --- four core questions, run as a team competition with a live results reveal
  \item \textbf{Takeaway:} practical tools for saving, investing, and beating inflation
\end{itemize}

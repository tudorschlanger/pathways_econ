% About This Workshop
% =============================================================================
\section*{About This Workshop}

This workshop uses real-life and policy examples to show how economics shapes the world around you --- from everyday choices to climate change, global trade, and financial markets. No prior economics knowledge required.

% =============================================================================
% What You'll Leave With
% =============================================================================
\section*{What You'll Leave With}

The ability to apply economic thinking to real decisions and policies --- and to spot the hidden trade-offs, data tricks, and mental shortcuts that shape how people (and markets) behave.

% =============================================================================
% Course Schedule
% =============================================================================
\section*{Course Schedule}

\textit{90 minutes per session}

% ---------- Day 1 ----------
\noindent\textbf{Day 1 --- Economic Decision Making \& Everyday Choices}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Concepts:} trade-offs, opportunity cost, sunk costs, incentives
  \item \textbf{Activity:} Weekend Planning --- design the best weekend under real constraints
  \item \textbf{Policy debate:} Should New Haven schools ban cell phones?
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.6em}

% ---------- Day 2 ----------
\noindent\textbf{Day 2 --- Economics \& Data}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Concepts:} correlation vs.\ causation, confounding variables, selection bias
  \item \textbf{Game:} work through a classic confounding-variable puzzle
  \item \textbf{Activity:} Chart Detective --- analyze real misleading charts
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.6em}

% ---------- Day 3 ----------
\noindent\textbf{Day 3 --- Climate Change \& Common Resources}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Concepts:} common resources, externalities, tragedy of the commons, government policy tools
  \item \textbf{Game:} Candy Commons --- experience the tragedy of the commons
  \item \textbf{Policy debate:} Should the U.S.\ impose a carbon tax?
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.6em}

% ---------- Day 4 ----------
\noindent\textbf{Day 4 --- Trade \& Tariffs}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Concepts:} gains from trade, imports and exports, tariffs
  \item \textbf{Game:} Candy Trading --- experience why voluntary trade makes both sides better off
  \item \textbf{Policy debate:} Are U.S.\ steel tariffs worth it? Read real sources and defend a position
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.6em}

% ---------- Day 5 ----------
\noindent\textbf{Day 5 --- Financial Literacy}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Concepts:} compound interest, inflation, diversification, credit cards, behavioral biases in spending
  \item \textbf{Activity:} The Credit Card Challenge --- compare repayment strategies and see how interest compounds
  \item \textbf{Takeaway:} practical tools for saving, borrowing, and spending smarter
\end{itemize}

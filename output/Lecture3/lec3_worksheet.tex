% ---------- Background ----------
\begin{definitionbox}[Carbon Tax]
  A fixed price charged for every \textbf{ton of CO\textsubscript{2}} emitted.
  The polluter pays for the damage their emissions cause.
  For example, the government sets a price of \textbf{\$50 per ton} of CO\textsubscript{2}, and firms and households pay when they emit.
\end{definitionbox}

\vspace{0.5em}

\textbf{Key Facts}
\begin{itemize}
  \item A \$50/ton carbon tax would raise gas prices from \$3.50 to \textbf{\$3.94 per gallon} (about 13\% more).
  \item The tax affects \textbf{firms} (higher production costs) and \textbf{households} (higher energy bills).
  \item It affects people \textbf{unequally}: low-income households spend a larger share of their income on energy.
  \item A carbon tax generates \textbf{billions of dollars} in revenue. The government can:
    \begin{enumerate}
      \item Return it to households as rebate checks
      \item Invest in firms to reduce CO\textsubscript{2}
      \item Cut other taxes (e.g.\ income tax)
    \end{enumerate}
\end{itemize}

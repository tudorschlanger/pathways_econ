\section*{Context}
% =============================================================================

In August 2022, the U.S.\ government signed the \textbf{Inflation Reduction Act}.
The table below shows the monthly inflation rate (CPI, year-over-year \% change)
for all of 2022.

\vspace{0.5em}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{l c l c}
  \toprule
  \textbf{Month} & \textbf{Inflation (\%)} & \textbf{Month} & \textbf{Inflation (\%)} \\
  \midrule
  January   & 7.5 & July      & 8.5 \\
  February  & 7.9 & August    & 8.3 \\
  March     & 8.5 & September & 8.2 \\
  April     & 8.3 & October   & 7.7 \\
  May       & 8.6 & November  & 7.1 \\
  June      & \textbf{9.1} (peak) & December  & 6.5 \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\vspace{0.3em}
\noindent\textbf{Key date:} The Inflation Reduction Act was signed on \textbf{August 16, 2022}.

\subsection*{Confounders}
% =============================================================================

\begin{definitionbox}[Confounder]
  A \textbf{hidden third factor} that drives both things you are comparing. It makes you think A causes B, when in reality something else (C) causes both A and B.
\end{definitionbox}

\vspace{0.5em}

Inflation Reduction Act \textbf{caused} the decline. List \textbf{three other

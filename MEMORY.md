## Project Context

[LEARN:context] Pathways is a 5-day Economics & Finance workshop for high school students at Yale University. Beamer-only output (no Quarto, no R). Separate handout .tex documents for interactive activities.

[LEARN:context] Existing reference materials live in `Lectures/` (PowerPoint, Word, Excel for 5 lessons) and `Pilot/` (pilot session materials). Instructor notes are in `notes.md`.

## Design Decisions

[LEARN:design] Beamer is the single source of truth for slides; Handouts/ contains standalone .tex activity documents.

[LEARN:design] Starting with Emory Beamer theme defaults (blue #012169, gold #B9975B, yellow #F2A900); Yale colors may be applied later.

[LEARN:design] Interactive activities (commons game, stock market game, debates) are in separate Handouts .tex files, not embedded in lecture slides.

## Workflow

[LEARN:workflow] Plan-first for all non-trivial tasks. Contractor mode after approval — coordinate autonomously, check in for ambiguity only.
